This page publishes materials such as Lean source code that cannot be withdrawn once in a public repository; some papers are under review and must not be de-anonymised — thank you for your understanding.

A boundary that must be stated. Mathematical theorems and formulae are themselves not protected by copyright. What is licensed here is expression, and the machine-checkable proof scripts — not the mathematical facts. No monopoly over those is claimed or possible.

AI use disclosure

Large language models served as a drafting tool that produced candidate code for the Lean 4 formal proofs and the three Python verification scripts. Every machine-checked conclusion rests on re-running the compiler and the independent scripts — never on the identity of the generator. A conclusion is machine-checked because the check passes, not because of who or what wrote it.

Why grades rather than judgement calls. Every rule on this page is enforced by a script at build time: the status gate, the anonymity scan, the licence check, the hash re-computation, and a red line that fails the build if a withheld item's title or venue appears anywhere in the output. A rule that lives in someone's memory is not a rule.
