Core hall · the seven pillars that survived

Decisive affirmative results, with the negatives that shaped them alongside
Latest round (round 69): the self-reference boundary machine — see Current programme.
Each pillar has two parts: the affirmative result, then the scaffold — the negative results that pressed it into that shape. The scaffold is not a list of failures; it is the mould. Detailed text is in Chinese pending translation.

H1 · 对象层

The boundary is the fixed-point set of a closure operator

Machine-checked evidence：第70轮 `fix_c_eq_decided` · 第72轮 `isClosure_leastAbove` ＋三实例 · 第73轮 偏序↔范畴字典（EM 代数 ⇔ 不动点）· 第61轮 `FixV_eq_range`：不动点＝右伴随的像（显式描述）

`Fix(c)` is the boundary; and three apparently different closure operators are three instances of the same construction (the `leastAbove` map) — the poset case and the category case have a correspondence dictionary.
H2 · 值层

The form of the potential is forced — but only half of it

Machine-checked evidence：第62轮 `V_global_min` / `V_gt_near_zero` / `V_gt_atTop` · 专题「靶心3」`value_layer_form_forced` · P4 达成：值层代数核 Lean 编译通过

The entropy term is a theorem: `H(κ) = C_F/κ`, monotone decreasing in κ (weight-(−1) equivariance of the rescaling law). κ★ is the unique global minimum of V, with ε–δ limits at both ends. `value_layer_form_forced` is machine-checked.
H3 · 三成分

“Fuzzy” and “uncertain” are two different things (the former word is retired)

Machine-checked evidence：第63轮 `pU_le_b` / `b_sub_pU_eq_two_min` / `b_eq_pU_iff` / `core_b_eq`（Lean exit 0）

The three-component field has been separated: `p_U ≤ b`, and `b − p_U = 2·min(p_S, p_N)`; `b = p_U` iff p_S or p_N is zero. b is a balance measure, not an uncertainty measure.
H4 · 量纲

The anchoring freedom is exactly one, checked on both sides

Machine-checked evidence：第77轮 `anchoring_fixes_one_degree` · 第78轮 `fix_rescale_image` / `anchoring_freedom_done_both_sides`

The value-layer scaling structure is machine-checked: anchoring fixes exactly one overall scale, one degree of freedom; the closure-side equivariance has also been supplied — `anchoring_freedom_done_both_sides`.
H5 · 读出

The intrinsic readout exists, is unique, and is independent of F

Machine-checked evidence：第107轮 `R_fixed_exists` / `R_fixed_unique` / `R_indep_of_F` · 第109轮 `n1_n2_n3_certificate` · 第111轮 `unitRead_scale_center_invariant` / `unitRead_agrees_reciprocal` / `unitRead_unique`

Reading one's own kernel in one's own unit scale gives `2/(1+e)`: independent of the scale center, equal to the reciprocal route's value, unique. Existence, uniqueness and independence of F for R(w)=1/w are all machine-checked; the N1/N2/N3 certificates are packaged.
H6 · 水平

No gauge-invariant identification exists — a decisive negative result

Machine-checked evidence：第116轮 `gaugeInvPrinciple_notSelects` / `selfConsistency_selects` · 第120轮 `no_gaugeInv_identification`（主结果）· 第114轮 `unitRead_gaugeInv_level_blind`

This is one of this line's own main theorems, and it is negative: no gauge-invariant selection principle can pick a level; anything that can pick one must break gauge invariance. The four T-θ‴ items are concluded, two of them negative theorems.
H7 · 可证伪

This line has, for the first time, a shape that an experiment can break

Machine-checked evidence：第86轮 `witness_iff_b_crosses` / `protocol_core` · 第119轮 `level_geometric` ＋ `falsifier_overshoot` / `falsifier_nonmonotone` / `falsifier_stalls`

The S4 failure condition has been formalized: an exponential-approach prediction plus three named refutations — overshoot / fall-back / stall. The criterion is lowered from "a statement about an operator" to "a statement about a measurable scalar": witness ⇔ there exists a b-crossing state.
