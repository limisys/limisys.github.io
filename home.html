The theory in four steps

The programme is not an optimisation trick. It is a chain from a philosophical concept to a machine-checked theorem:

philosophical concept→ formal mathematical object→ computable observable→ machine-checked theorem
Step 1 · Ontology What is a boundary? An agent's autonomy boundary is defined as the stable closure fiber of an attribution functor on a category of causal interventions. This is a definition, not a measurement. Definition
Step 2 · Geometry Give the boundary a coordinate: sharpness κ The object is projected onto a single scalar — how sharply the boundary discriminates. κ is what makes the object comparable across systems. Definition
Step 3 · Dynamics Two costs, one potential Maintenance cost grows with sharpness; uncertainty entropy falls with it. Their sum is a potential whose minimum is the equilibrium sharpness. Machine-Checked
Step 4 · Observable A criterion you could measure At the equilibrium the dimensionless ratio σ takes a definite value. Whether real agents exhibit it is an empirical question that has not been tested. Empirical prediction
Read this before the mathematics. The formal layer is built and machine-checked. The empirical layer is empty: no real deployed agent system has been tested. Every page states which of the five labels applies — Definition, Assumption, Machine-Checked Theorem, Conjecture, Empirical Prediction.
