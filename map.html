Research map

The programme's global map of research lines

This figure shows where each research line of the programme currently stands — its programmatic premise, the formalisation work, the evidence behind it, and how it reaches interpretation and application. It is a map of the work, not a single theorem chain.

The four layers

Layer 1 Research starting point A programmatic premise — the core of self-consciousness as identifying, representing and maintaining the self / non-self boundary. This is not a Lean theorem.
Layer 2 Four formalisation lines A | Boundary potential (value layer); B | Boundary–self-report (conditional identity); C | Level self-reference (a specified selector); D | Assembly at the closure face.
Layer 3 Evidence Each line's theorem, counterexample, or unfinished item: Lean proofs, numerical cross-checks, the Level 2 toy machine, and open construction problems.
Layer 4 Interpretation and application Kant companion reading, engineering direction, and empirical direction — philosophical or engineering / empirical proposals, not conclusions derived automatically from the theorems.

What the arrows mean

StyleMeaning
SolidEvidence chain of the corresponding research line.
DashedResearch motivation / conceptual link — not a mathematical derivation.
DottedInterpretation or application direction.

Status note

This map describes the state as of 2026-10-19. Counts for the unified repository and the archival status of the Level 2 machine follow each fixed version and its release record.
